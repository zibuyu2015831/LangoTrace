当前句讲解

spent a long time 比 stayed for a long time 更自然地表达“度过一段时间”。

词句提取

drizzling, in no hurry, listened to the rain, watched people passing by.

相似记忆

你去年写过“在车站等雨停”，可以复用 listening to the rain。

The Coffee Cart Economy

Every morning at seven, a small coffee cart rolls up to the corner outside our office building. The owner, a quiet man in his fifties, sets up his grinder, lines up his cups, and waits. He never advertises. He says the smell does the work for him.

What makes the cart interesting is not the coffee itself, but the way it changes the street. Strangers who normally rush past each other start to form a loose, patient line. Regulars learn each other's orders, and someone always holds a spot for the nurse who gets off the night shift. The cart runs on a kind of word of mouth economy: nobody promotes it online, yet the line grows a little longer every week.

Economists like to study markets with prices and contracts, but most of daily life runs on softer rules. A cart that shows up on time, a vendor who remembers your name — these small reliabilities are worth more than any loyalty program, and they are almost impossible to copy.

word of mouth

指「口口相传、口碑」，强调信息靠人与人之间的口头传播。这里说咖啡车靠 word of mouth economy 运转，即完全由口碑驱动、没有任何线上宣传的小生意。

设计说明

  • 原生选择手势：取词复用 Apple 系统选择——双击选单词、长按拖动选片段、三击选整句；不在正文上叠加逐句按钮，保持连续阅读的排版。
  • 150ms debounce：选择稳定 150ms 后才触发学习面板，避免拖动选择过程中面板内容反复闪动。
  • 新选择先 collapsed：产生新选择时面板先以收起态出现（只显示选中文本与展开入口），不直接展开上一个片段的旧解释误导用户。
  • 显式发送边界：解释请求只发送选中片段及必要的上下文窗口，不自动发送全文；面板出现本身不发起 AI 请求，由用户的选择动作显式触发。
  • 不自动 TTS：「听」需要点击才播放选中片段；打开文档、滚动、选择都不会自动发声。
  • 持久高亮：浅黄高亮标记此前查过的片段，重读时可直接点击唤回解释；高亮数据保存在本机。

Why Street Coffee Carts Survive

导入于 6月10日 · 982 词 · 读到 36%

The first time I noticed the coffee cart outside our office, I assumed it would be gone within a month. Street vendors come and go in this part of the city, and a cart that sells nothing but espresso seemed like a fragile business.

I was wrong. Three months later the cart is still there, and the line in front of it is often longer than the one inside the chain café across the street. The owner roasts his own beans, remembers people's orders, and leaves at exactly eleven o'clock every morning.

Part of the appeal is scarcity. Because the cart only stays for three hours a day, regulars have carved out a small ritual 用语迹解释 拷贝 查询系统词典 around it: arrive before the morning meeting, exchange a few words with the owner, and carry the cup upstairs while it is still warm.

There is a lesson here that has little to do with coffee. A small business does not need to win every hour of the day. It needs a moment that belongs to it, and the patience to show up for that moment again and again.

解释

carve out 原义是「凿出、切出」，引申为花心思「开辟、经营出」一块属于自己的东西。carved out a small ritual 指常客们围绕这辆咖啡车，慢慢养成了一套属于自己的小小固定仪式。ritual 在这里不是宗教仪式，而是日常里有仪式感的固定习惯。
